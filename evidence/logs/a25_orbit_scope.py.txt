==============================================================================
A25.3  which redundancy breaks averaging
==============================================================================
  multiplicative (a,w)->(ta,w/t):  own 1.78e-15  aggregate shift 0.4036
  additive     (u,v)->(u+c,v-c):  own 3.55e-15  aggregate shift 7.245e-16

  Both are exact orbits; only the multiplicative one survives
  averaging, because averaging commutes with a common translation
  and not with a common rescaling.  eq:homog is multiplicative.

and not linearity.  sec:gauge must say 'linear regressor', and the
Kalman filter cannot serve as the orbit-free control it was used as.
