A23.2  does M1 still bind here, and does frame mixing reach it?
==============================================================================
  V3.3's M1 rows were all inert at 6.85x -- its floor never bound, so
  that harness could not rank the mechanisms.  Here:

  best M1 row          1.00x fair
  best frame-mix row   4.10x fair
  best weight-mix row  1.00x fair
  M1 range across the three floors: 1.56x, so the floor BINDS.
  M1 is load-bearing and the frame mix is not, which is the design rule
  sec:e3 states -- now supported by rows that share a baseline.

A23.3  the anchor-budget sweep, with seeds
==============================================================================
  V3.2 swept a on ONE seed and eps rose 0.1487 -> 0.1817 against its
  own prediction of a 1/sqrt(a) fall; sec:e3 calls it flat.  Eight
  seeds, E3's harness, lam = 0.5, frame mixing:

   a    x fair       eps   sd(eps)  eps*sqrt(a)
   1     4.46x    0.1337    0.0166       0.1337
   2     4.76x    0.1494    0.0178       0.2113
   4     5.09x    0.1542    0.0239       0.3084
   8     5.04x    0.1532    0.0156       0.4333
  16     5.08x    0.1603    0.0229       0.6414
  32     5.09x    0.1695    0.0321       0.9591
  64     5.14x    0.1650    0.0193       1.3200

  log eps vs log a slope +0.0476; the prediction eps ~ 1/sqrt(a)
  needs -0.5, and a flat eps needs 0.
  spread 0.0358 against a mean seed sd of 0.0212, i.e.
  1.7 seed sd.
  The variation across a is inside the seed noise, so 'flat' is the
  honest description and the 1/sqrt(a) term is not detectable here.
  Either way the load-bearing claim is the one that survives: under
  TSP-Var the anchor budget does not govern displacement, Pi_max does.
