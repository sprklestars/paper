==============================================================================
A8.3  the directions normalisation ADDS (no plain-network analogue)
==============================================================================
  Scaling or shifting the pre-normalisation activations of a
  normalised layer changes nothing downstream, and unlike the
  per-unit scale it requires NO compensating change elsewhere.

           move on layer 0    max |df|   param move  agg x clean
           scale W,b by 10     1.4e-09         5.17         1.02
          scale W,b by 100     1.4e-09        56.86         1.06
             shift b by +5     2.0e-15         2.03         1.00
            shift b by +50     1.7e-14        20.29         1.00
   scale 100 then shift 50     1.4e-09        60.38         1.06

  The scale move is exact only up to LayerNorm's epsilon, so its
  df is small-but-nonzero; the shift is exact.  Either way no
  functional audit at any realistic tolerance sees these.

==============================================================================
A8.4  does weight decay suppress the gauge?
==============================================================================
  Weight decay penalises large weights, and the gauge can make
  weights large, so it plausibly pins the gauge down.  But decay is
  applied during TRAINING, by the client, and the gauge move is
  applied to what the client SUBMITS.  Measured both ways.

 weight decay  client own MSE  param norm  agg x clean  own MSE delta
        0e+00        0.629016       11.43         2.85       0.00e+00
        1e-04        0.628670       11.43         2.84       0.00e+00
        1e-03        0.625408       11.38         2.81       3.70e-17
        1e-02        0.597547       10.90         2.52       0.00e+00

  Decay shrinks the honest weights, which is visible in column 3,
  and does nothing to the gap: the adversary applies the gauge after
  training, and the server cannot verify that decay was used.
