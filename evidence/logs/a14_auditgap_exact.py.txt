A14.2  continuity of D on (0, inf)
==============================================================================

D is a composition of continuous maps (parameter average, relu,
squared error), so continuity is not in doubt analytically.  What is
worth checking is that nothing in the harness introduces a jump --
a clipped unit, an overflow -- at the scales the proof ranges over.

grid: 200 points, a in [0.500, 500.0]
D range on grid:            [0.9999, 8.4654]
max relative step to next:  0.0691
median relative step:       0.00044

steps shrink with grid spacing, which is what continuity means
operationally here; no jump discontinuity is present.
