==============================================================================
A15 conclusion: (C4) against the full four-detector suite caps
TSP-Gauge damage at a median 1.00x, against the
unconstrained magnitudes the paper quotes.  Those magnitudes are
the reach of the GROUP, which is what prop:auditgap is about; the
silent figure is the reach of an adversary facing distance-based
monitoring.  Both belong in the paper, labelled as what they are.
The composed attack the abstract quotes has median 789948x undefended
(range 943-63262020x over 8 seeds) and median 1.00x under M4+.
