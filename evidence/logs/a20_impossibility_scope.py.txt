A20.5  the repaired statement, as a predicate
==============================================================================
  Claim: with a = 0, for every translation-equivariant scorer, the
  suppression applied to a common-mode-biased client in W1 equals the
  displacement inflicted on the origin-tracking honest client in W2;
  hence a scorer that suppresses at all (supp < 1) has eps > 0, and one
  that excludes (supp = 0) has eps = 1.  No scorer both suppresses and
  keeps eps = 0.
