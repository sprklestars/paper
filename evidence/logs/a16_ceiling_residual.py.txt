A16  the residual in prop:ceiling, attributed
==============================================================================

het = honest sigmas uniform(0.8,1.2) as in C3d; hom = common sigma_H,
which is the case the proof states.  T is raised with rho so that the
EMA is equally converged in every column.

    N  eq:ceil  N/(2N-1)  het rho.9  hom rho.9  hom rho.99  hom rho.999
----------------------------------------------------------------------
   10   0.5235    0.5263     0.5115     0.5236      0.5231       0.5235
   20   0.5122    0.5128     0.4963     0.5069      0.5119       0.5120
   30   0.5082    0.5085     0.4996     0.5115      0.5084       0.5082
   50   0.5049    0.5051     0.4910     0.5023      0.5027       0.5047
  100   0.5025    0.5025     0.4832     0.4975      0.5015       0.5027

residual with heterogeneity: 0.9 to 1.9 percentage points, growing in N
residual without it, rho=0.999: 0.03 percentage points worst case, no trend

So (a) accounts for it and (b) does not: rho from 0.9 to 0.999
changes nothing, while removing the spread in sigma_H closes the gap
to a fiftieth of a point at every N.  prop:ceiling is exact for the
common-sigma case it states, and the measured curve sits below it
because 1/sigma^2 is convex, so dispersion among the honest clients
inflates their total weight.  The direction is favourable to the
defender and the ceiling remains ~N/2 times a fair share.
