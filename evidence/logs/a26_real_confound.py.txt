A26.1  is dispersion correlated with bias on REAL shards?
==============================================================================
  rho > 0 means a client that looks stable is also a client that
  is close to the ideal update -- the confound sec:e8 asserts.
  The synthetic row is the control: there they are drawn
  independently, so its |rho| is what sampling noise alone gives.

  partition                  N   rho(sigma^2, bias)  rho(rank)
  california (geography)    12                0.711      0.420
  digits (label skew)       10                0.554      0.152
  synthetic (control)       30                0.017      0.032

  The control is the null: |rho| = 0.017 at N=30 from
  sampling alone.  A real partition exceeding it has the confound.

==============================================================================
  Here bias and dispersion are independent by construction, so a

A26 conclusion
==============================================================================
  A26.1  correlation |rho|: real up to 0.711 against a
         synthetic control of 0.017.  The confound is real
         and POSITIVE: quiet clients are accurate clients.

  partition        N   x fair  abs share    damage
  california      12    7.78x      0.648    1.087x
  digits          10    6.91x      0.691    1.005x
  synthetic       30   20.37x      0.679    4.114x

  Read the ABSOLUTE share column, not the multiple: a multiple of
  fair share is not comparable across N, and at these three N the
  adversary ends up holding the same fraction of the aggregate.
  At matched share the weight transfers to real data and the
  damage does not.  On a partition where dispersion tracks
  accuracy, TSP-Var captures a client the aggregate WANTS
  overweighted, so a damage figure measured there would
  understate the attack rather than demonstrate it.  That is the
  confound sec:e8 asserts, measured, and it is why the attack is
  reported on the design that draws bias and dispersion
  independently.
