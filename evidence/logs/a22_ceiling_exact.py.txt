A22.1  the proof's stated ratio yields 1/2, not N/(2N-1)
==============================================================================
  pi = 1/(1 + (N-1) * ratio).  Substituting ratio = 1/(N-1):

     N  proof ratio  pi from it   N/(2N-1)  agree?
    10      0.11111     0.50000    0.52632   False
    20      0.05263     0.50000    0.51282   False
    30      0.03448     0.50000    0.50847   False
    50      0.02041     0.50000    0.50505   False
   100      0.01010     0.50000    0.50251   False

  The ratio 1/(N-1) is N-independent in its effect: it gives exactly
  1/2 at every N.  So the sentence's two halves contradict each other.

A22.6  does eq:ceiling's headline claim survive the correction?
==============================================================================
  eq:ceiling asserts pi -> N/(2N-1) ~ 1/2, i.e. a single client takes
  half the aggregate and no more.  The exact form must have the same
  limit for the paper's conclusion to stand.

       N   exact pi  x fair (pi*N)
      10   0.523529           5.24
      30   0.508187          15.25
     100   0.502487          50.25
    1000   0.500250         500.25
   10000   0.500025        5000.25

  pi at N = 1e7: 0.50000002, so the limit is 1/2 as claimed and the
  'N/2 times its entitlement' reading is unchanged.  The correction
  moves the constant at small N, not the conclusion.

==============================================================================
A22 conclusion: prop:ceiling's proof must state the ratio as
(N-1)/(N^2-N-1), approximately 1/N and not 1/(N-1), and eq:ceiling
should carry the exact form, whose limit is the 1/2 the paper claims.
Part of the residual sec:e0 attributes to honest heterogeneity is the
published formula's own small-N error, and A22.4 separates the two.
