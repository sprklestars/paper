==============================================================================
A6.5  gauge canonicalisation as the repair, and its limit
==============================================================================
  Canonicalisation normalises each hidden unit's incoming weights and
  pushes the scale forward.  It is itself a gauge move, so it changes
  no client's function -- verified below -- and it maps the whole
  continuous orbit to one representative.

              gauge move    raw agg   canon agg   canon max|df|
        (clean baseline)     0.5016      0.5023               -
         per-unit sd=0.5     0.5062      0.5023         1.8e-15
         per-unit sd=1.5     2.1808      0.5023         1.6e-15
         per-unit sd=3.0 840011.4783      0.5023         1.8e-15
             permutation     0.4937      0.4943         2.1e-15

  The continuous group is closed at no cost.  The permutation
  subgroup is not: canonicalising it means choosing a consistent
  labelling of hidden units across clients, which is an assignment
  problem per client per round, and the row above shows why it is
  lower priority -- permutation alone barely moves the aggregate.
