==============================================================================
A7.4  the control: a linear model at matched parameter count
==============================================================================
  The network's gap grows with capacity.  A linear model has no
  orbit at ANY dimension, so growing it must not open a gap.

  dimension     own MSE   own MSE after  agg x clean
         20    0.001071       13.262334       417.19
        100    0.009511       10.572966        67.06
        500    0.010646        9.821933        40.26
       2000    0.009497        8.984016        35.00

  Own MSE moves with the aggregate at every dimension: the audit
  always sees it.  Capacity is not the mechanism; the ORBIT is.
