  16   0.50 |             1.5429

  At lam=1 the frame sits on the biased consensus and the common-mode bias is
  invisible, exactly as Theorem 2 predicts. Only lam<1 pulls the frame back,
  and the residual error falls with a. THIS is where the anchor budget binds.

V4.3  M1 must be scaled: floor as a fraction of median dispersion
================================================================================
  An absolute floor is not scale-free -- it depends on the learning
  rate and the update magnitude. A relative floor is.

  floor / median disp |  adv share   x fair      eps |  honest MSE cost
                 none |     0.2121    6.36x   0.1849 |           1.003x
                 0.05 |     0.2121    6.36x   0.1849 |           1.003x
                 0.10 |     0.1924    5.77x   0.1645 |           1.003x
                 0.20 |     0.1112    3.34x   0.0806 |           1.003x
                 0.30 |     0.0786    2.36x   0.0468 |           1.002x
                 0.50 |     0.0556    1.67x   0.0230 |           1.032x
                 0.80 |     0.0437    1.31x   0.0108 |           1.133x
                 1.00 |     0.0394    1.18x   0.0062 |           1.207x

  Column 5 is the aggregate's variance relative to the BLUE optimum on an
  all-honest population: the price of disbelieving low variance estimates.
  A relative floor removes the capture and the cost is bounded.
