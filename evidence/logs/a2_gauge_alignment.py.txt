A2.5  is the obvious fix -- normalise before scoring -- sufficient?
==========================================================================
  Canonicalisation candidates:
    raw       score the parameter vector as submitted
    l2norm    rescale whole vector to unit norm (norm clipping)
    layerwise rescale each layer to unit norm (fixes S1)

  poison                raw             l2norm          layerwise
               before/after       before/after       before/after
     2.0           30 -> 29           29 -> 30           22 -> 22
     5.0           30 -> 30           30 -> 30           30 -> 30
    10.0           30 -> 30           30 -> 30           30 -> 30

  Layerwise normalisation quotients out S1 but NOT S2 (permutation),
  which has no cheap canonical form -- matching hidden units across
  clients is the assignment problem.
